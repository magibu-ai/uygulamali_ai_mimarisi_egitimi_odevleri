Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 27, 2026

Ezan Vakti ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our mobile application handles your information.

1. Information We Collect

Our application is designed with user privacy in mind. We do not require user registration, login, or account creation.

  • Location Data: The app requests approximate/precise location permission solely to calculate accurate prayer times based on your current geographical coordinates. Location data is processed locally on your device or sent directly to the prayer times API to fetch times, and it is never stored on or transmitted to any developer servers.
  • Local Storage (SharedPreferences): The app stores your preferences locally on your device (such as selected city, district, notification settings, and theme preferences). This data never leaves your device.

2. Third-Party Services

Prayer Times API: The app uses the public AlAdhan API to fetch daily prayer times. Only your location coordinates or region parameters are transmitted over HTTPS to retrieve these times.

3. Permissions

  • Location: Used strictly for calculating prayer times.
  • Notifications: Used locally via flutter_local_notifications to remind you of prayer times.
  • Internet: Used exclusively to fetch prayer times from the AlAdhan API.

4. Data Sharing

We do not collect, store, share, or sell any personal data to third parties. There are no analytics tools, crash reporters, or advertising networks (such as Google AdMob or Firebase) integrated into the app.

5. Children's Privacy

Our app does not knowingly collect any personal information from children under the age of 13.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
